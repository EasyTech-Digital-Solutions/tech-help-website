How We Work With Seniors

EasyTech's approach to senior tech support is different from a typical computer repair shop. We come to you. There's no need to bring a device anywhere or wait in a queue. We sit down together at your kitchen table or living room and work through the problem side by side, explaining each step in plain English without any technical jargon.

We understand that technology changes quickly and that instructions which seem obvious to younger users are not always intuitive the first time around. Our technicians are trained to be patient and to repeat or rephrase anything until it makes sense. We encourage questions, and we don't make you feel rushed. After each visit, we can leave you with a simple written cheat sheet (in large print if helpful) so you have something to refer back to when we're gone.

Privacy and respect are important to us. We never handle your accounts or passwords without your permission, and we explain exactly what we're doing before we do it. Many of our senior clients in Coquitlam, Burnaby, Surrey, Vancouver, and Maple Ridge have been with EasyTech for multiple visits because they know they're getting honest, trustworthy help from someone who genuinely cares about their experience with technology.

Senior Services

Phone & Tablet Help

Smartphones and tablets are incredibly useful for staying connected with family, but they can be confusing when you're just starting out or when an update changes where everything is. EasyTech helps with the full range of phone and tablet tasks: setting up WhatsApp and FaceTime so you can video call grandchildren, organizing your contacts and photo albums, backing up your photos to the cloud so they're never lost, and adjusting settings like font size, display brightness, and notification sounds to suit your preferences. We work with both iPhone and Android devices and take as much time as needed.

Computer & Laptop Support

Computers slow down over time, and older operating systems often stop receiving security updates, which puts your personal information at risk. EasyTech helps seniors with email account setup and recovery, Windows and macOS updates, performance cleanup to speed up sluggish computers, and printer connections. We also show you safe browsing habits: how to identify suspicious websites, how to avoid downloading unwanted software, and how to recognize when a pop-up warning is a scam rather than a real problem.

Smart TV & Home Tech

Modern Smart TVs offer access to Netflix, YouTube, CBC Gem, and dozens of other streaming services, but navigating the menus and getting everything connected can be frustrating without guidance. EasyTech sets up your Smart TV, installs and organizes the streaming apps you want to use, connects your soundbar or speaker system, and programs your remote so you only need one device to control everything. We also add streaming sticks like Apple TV or Roku to older sets.

Online Safety & Scam Protection

Seniors are disproportionately targeted by online scams, phone fraud, and fake tech support calls. EasyTech's online safety sessions teach you exactly what real banks, government agencies, and tech companies will and won't ever ask you to do, so you can recognize a scam immediately. We set up strong passwords, a simple password manager, two-factor authentication, and reputable security software to keep your accounts protected.

Help Choosing What to Buy

Not sure which phone, tablet, or computer to get? Before you spend money, EasyTech will walk you through your options in plain language and recommend something that's actually easy to use, without paying for features you'll never touch.
