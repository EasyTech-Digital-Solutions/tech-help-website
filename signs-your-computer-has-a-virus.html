How to Tell If Your Computer Has a Virus

Not every slow computer has a virus — but ignoring the real warning signs can turn a five-minute cleanup into a full data-loss emergency. Here's what to actually watch for, how to check safely, and when it's time to stop and call someone.

Sudden browser redirects. If your browser starts opening to a search engine or homepage you didn't set, or search results take you somewhere unexpected, that's a strong sign of browser hijacking malware — usually picked up from a bundled software installer.

Pop-up ads, especially ones that won't close. Occasional ads are normal browsing life. Persistent pop-ups that reappear after closing, especially ones warning about "infections" and urging you to call a number or download software, are themselves the infection — never call the number or click through.

Programs launching on their own. If applications open without you starting them, or your webcam light turns on when you're not using it, that's worth investigating immediately — it can indicate remote access malware.

A fan running constantly at full speed. Malware often runs background processes that max out your CPU, and the extra heat makes your fan work overtime even when you're not doing anything demanding.

How to check on Windows: Open Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) and look at the CPU and Memory columns. An unfamiliar process consistently using over 30% of your CPU with nothing else running is suspicious and worth researching by name before deciding whether to end it.

What to do about it. Free tools like Malwarebytes can scan for and remove most common infections in under ten minutes, and are a reasonable first step for straightforward cases. However, rootkits and browser hijackers are often built specifically to survive a normal scan and may require a full system reset to fully clear.

If you're seeing banking-related warnings or think your accounts may be compromised, stop using the computer for financial tasks immediately — don't log into banking, email, or anything with saved passwords until it's been checked.
