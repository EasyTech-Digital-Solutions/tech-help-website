Our Business IT Support Services

Network Security & Setup

Your business network is the foundation everything else depends on. EasyTech sets up secure, segmented Wi-Fi with separate guest and staff networks, configures firewalls, and implements VPN access so employees can connect safely from home or on the road. We conduct basic cybersecurity assessments to identify weak points — default router passwords, unencrypted file shares, outdated firmware — and fix them before they become problems.

Computer & Device Support

When a computer crashes or slows to a crawl, it costs your business real time and money. EasyTech provides fast, on-site troubleshooting for Windows and Mac computers, handles software installation and licensing, and performs hardware upgrades like RAM and SSD replacements that can dramatically extend the life of older machines. We also set up remote desktop tools and handle device retirement and data wiping. Most issues are resolved in a single visit.

Microsoft 365 & Cloud Solutions

EasyTech handles the full M365 migration and configuration — Exchange Online email with your business domain, Teams for communication and video meetings, SharePoint and OneDrive for shared file storage, and security policies through the Admin Center. We manage user onboarding and offboarding, and handle data migration from Gmail or a local server with minimal downtime.

Email & Exchange Configuration

EasyTech sets up business email through Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or other hosted providers, and configures Outlook on all your devices. We transfer existing emails and contacts when migrating, and configure spam filtering, email signatures, distribution lists, and email security policies (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) to protect your domain reputation.

Cloud Storage & Backup Services

EasyTech sets up automated backup systems that run in the background without requiring staff to remember anything. We configure backups to OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox Business, or a local NAS device, and set up cloud storage with proper folder structures and sharing permissions. If you've never had a backup strategy, we'll audit your data and build one that fits your budget.

Employee Tech Training

EasyTech runs hands-on training sessions tailored to your team's actual day-to-day tasks — getting comfortable with Microsoft Teams, using OneDrive instead of USB drives, or spotting phishing emails. Training is done in plain language with real examples from your workplace. We can also create simple reference guides for staff desks.

Printer & Peripheral Setup

EasyTech installs and configures network printers so every device on your network can print without USB cables. We set up scanner-to-email functionality, connect multi-function devices to your file storage, and troubleshoot driver and connectivity issues. We also handle label printers, receipt printers for retail, and document cameras for meeting rooms.

IT Consulting & Planning

EasyTech offers free initial consultations to assess your current IT setup, identify gaps, and recommend a practical roadmap for improvement. Whether you're opening a second location, hiring more staff, or moving to a new office, we help you plan the technology side so there are no surprises. Our advice is honest and unbiased — not tied to any vendor.
